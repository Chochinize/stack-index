Firefly III is for people who want to really understand their money. It models accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, and categories with double-entry rigor, then gives you reports and insights — all on a server you run yourself.

It has a solid API and a data-importer that can pull transactions from banks (via community connectors) or files, so you get deep tracking without handing your financial life to a third party.

**Good to know**

- Docker deployment; needs a database.
- Powerful rules engine and reporting.
- Importer supports many banks and CSV formats.

Coolify gives you the push-to-deploy magic of Heroku or Vercel on your **own** hardware. Connect a server, link a Git repo, and it builds and runs your apps, provisions databases, manages TLS, and handles zero-downtime deploys — all from a friendly dashboard.

It's become the go-to for people who want modern developer ergonomics without the per-seat cloud bill or vendor lock-in. Your apps, your databases, your servers.

> A Heroku-style deploy flow that you fully control.

**Good to know**

- Deploys Docker/Compose apps, static sites, and databases.
- Automatic TLS, previews, and webhooks.
- Manages one server or a fleet.

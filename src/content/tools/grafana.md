Grafana turns Prometheus, Loki, InfluxDB, SQL, and dozens of other data sources into rich, interactive dashboards and alerts. It's the visualization layer at the heart of most self-hosted monitoring stacks, and it runs entirely on your own servers.

Own your observability instead of paying per-host to a SaaS to look at your own metrics.

**Good to know**
- Connects to virtually any metrics/logs source.
- Alerting and templated dashboards.
- The backbone of most homelab monitoring.

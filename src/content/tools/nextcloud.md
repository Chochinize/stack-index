Nextcloud is the Swiss-army platform of self-hosting: install it on a box you control and you get file sync and share, calendars, contacts, an online office suite, chat and video (Talk), and a huge app store on top.

Everything lives on **your** server, so your files, meetings, and contacts never touch a third-party cloud. Desktop and mobile clients keep devices in sync, and admins get fine-grained control over storage back-ends, encryption, and sharing policies.

> One deployment can replace Drive, Docs, Calendar, Contacts, and more — all self-hosted.

**Good to know**

- Easiest via the official Docker/AIO image; scales to serious deployments.
- AGPL-3.0, with an optional enterprise subscription for support.
- Pairs well with a reverse proxy + your own domain and TLS.

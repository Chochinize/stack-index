Excalidraw is the go-to for quick, hand-drawn-style diagrams and wireframes. By default your drawing lives locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded — and you can self-host the whole app plus a collaboration server if you want real-time boards on your own infrastructure.

It's fast, delightful, and keeps your sketches yours.

**Good to know**
- Local-first: drawings stay in your browser by default.
- Self-hostable app + optional collaboration server.
- Export to PNG/SVG; end-to-end encrypted live sessions.

Actual is a snappy zero-based budgeting app built around the idea that your financial data should stay yours. It runs local-first — the app works fully offline against a local database — and you can stand up your own sync server to share the budget across devices.

It brings serious YNAB-style envelope budgeting (rules, scheduled transactions, reports) without a monthly fee or a company holding your transaction history.

**Good to know**

- Local-first core; optional self-hosted sync server (Docker).
- Bank import via files or community sync integrations.
- Fully open source (MIT) after the project was open-sourced.

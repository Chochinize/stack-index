Vikunja is a flexible task and project manager you host yourself. The same tasks can be viewed as simple lists, kanban boards, tables, or a Gantt timeline, so it fits both personal to-dos and small-team projects.

It's fast, has a clean API, supports reminders and recurring tasks, and keeps all your plans on your own server rather than a productivity SaaS.

**Good to know**

- Single Go binary or container; SQLite/Postgres/MySQL back-ends.
- CalDAV support and a documented REST API.
- Multi-user with teams and sharing.

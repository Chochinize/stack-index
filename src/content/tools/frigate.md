Frigate watches your security cameras and runs real-time object detection locally, recording only what matters and alerting on people, cars, or animals — all without sending a single frame to a vendor's cloud. It pairs tightly with Home Assistant.

With a Coral or GPU it runs detection efficiently on modest hardware, so your camera footage stays entirely on your network.

**Good to know**
- Real-time local AI detection (Coral/GPU accelerated).
- Deep Home Assistant integration.
- Records events, not 24/7 noise.

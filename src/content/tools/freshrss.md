FreshRSS pulls your feeds into one fast, keyboard-friendly reader on your own server. It scales to hundreds of feeds, supports web scraping for sites without feeds, and exposes an API so you can use third-party mobile reader apps.

Reclaim the open web without a feed-reader company tracking what you read.

**Good to know**
- Handles hundreds of feeds smoothly.
- API for popular mobile RSS clients.
- Multi-user; low resource footprint.

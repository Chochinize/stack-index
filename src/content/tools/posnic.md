Posnic is an offline-first point-of-sale application for retail shops and restaurants. The desktop app stores its primary data on the till, so checkout and billing continue without a constant internet connection. A self-hosted browser edition is available for businesses that prefer to operate Posnic on infrastructure they control.

Its workflows cover products, inventory, customers, sales, returns, receipts, reports, restaurant orders, and register sessions. Inventory can be exported as CSV, and Posnic includes documented backup and restore workflows.

**Good to know**

- Stable desktop packages are published for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- Posnic's own source is AGPL-3.0-only; packaged dependencies retain their own licenses.
- Self-hosting does not provide automatic synchronization between tills or branches.
- Posnic records payment methods but is not a merchant acquirer or integrated card terminal.

Uptime Kuma is the self-hosted monitor that made "watch my services" genuinely pleasant. It pings HTTP endpoints, TCP ports, containers, and more, then shows a clean real-time dashboard and fires alerts through dozens of channels when something breaks.

It also builds public status pages, so you can host your own status.example.com — no monitoring subscription, and your uptime data stays in-house.

**Good to know**

- One container; supports HTTP, TCP, DNS, ping, and push checks.
- 90+ notification integrations (Telegram, Slack, webhooks…).
- Built-in status pages and maintenance windows.

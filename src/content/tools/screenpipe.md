Screenpipe records screen text and audio into a searchable local history. You can look up something you saw or heard, use captured context for meeting notes and work summaries, or let an AI assistant query it through the local API and [MCP integration](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe/tree/main/packages/screenpipe-mcp).

Recordings and the SQLite search index stay on your device by default. Local capture and search work offline after setup. Local transcription and local AI options are available, but configured cloud AI, transcription, sync, and integrations can send context off-device.

The source is available under the [Screenpipe Commercial License](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe/blob/main/LICENSE.md), rather than an OSI-approved open-source license. See the [official site](https://screenpipe.com) for current plans and the [README](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe#readme) for platform and setup details.

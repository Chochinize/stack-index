Navidrome indexes your own music files and streams them to a fast web player and to any Subsonic-compatible mobile app. It's a single lightweight Go binary (or container) that handles large libraries with ease.

Because it speaks the Subsonic API, you get a wide choice of polished mobile clients while keeping your actual music — and your listening history — entirely on your own server.

**Good to know**

- Tiny footprint; runs happily on a Raspberry Pi.
- Multi-user with per-user playlists and favorites.
- Works with many third-party Subsonic apps.

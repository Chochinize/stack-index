Planka recreates the familiar Trello experience — boards, lists, cards, labels, due dates, and real-time collaboration — as software you host yourself. Drag cards across lists, assign members, and attach files, all without a per-user SaaS bill.

For small teams that just want a shared kanban board without giving up their data, it's a tidy fit.

**Good to know**
- Real-time multi-user boards.
- Markdown cards, labels, and attachments.
- One Docker Compose stack to run.

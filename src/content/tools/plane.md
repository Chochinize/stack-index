Plane brings modern issue tracking to your own server: backlogs, sprints (cycles), modules, and roadmaps with a clean, fast interface. It's aimed at product and engineering teams who like Linear's ergonomics but want to self-host.

Your issues, specs, and roadmap stay on infrastructure you control, not a per-seat SaaS.

**Good to know**
- Cycles, modules, and roadmap views.
- Fast, keyboard-friendly UI.
- Self-hosted via Docker Compose.

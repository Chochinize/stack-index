Matrix is an open, federated protocol for real-time communication, and Synapse is the server you run to own your slice of it. Host it, connect the Element apps, and you have private group chat, voice, and video with end-to-end encryption — under your control, on your domain.

Because Matrix federates, your self-hosted server can still talk to the wider network, and bridges let you connect rooms to Slack, Discord, IRC, and more without giving up self-hosting.

**Good to know**

- Your homeserver holds your accounts, rooms, and history.
- E2EE by default for private conversations.
- Bridges keep you connected to non-Matrix networks.

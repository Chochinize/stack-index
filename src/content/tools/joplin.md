Joplin keeps your notes and to-dos in Markdown on your device, with optional end-to-end encrypted sync through storage you choose — your own Nextcloud, WebDAV, or S3. There's no mandatory account and no company reading your notebooks.

A web clipper, attachments, and a big plugin ecosystem make it a genuine Evernote replacement that respects your data.

**Good to know**
- Local-first Markdown; sync via your own backend.
- End-to-end encryption option.
- Web clipper + plugins + mobile apps.

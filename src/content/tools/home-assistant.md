Home Assistant is the hub that ties a smart home together **without** routing everything through a vendor's cloud. It integrates thousands of devices and services, and — crucially — runs your automations locally, so lights, locks, and sensors keep working even when your internet is down.

It's the flagship of local-first home automation: your presence data, camera feeds, and routines stay on your own hardware. A massive community means integrations for nearly everything, and the mobile apps give you remote control through your own tunnel.

> Local control first, cloud optional — privacy and reliability by design.

**Good to know**

- Runs on a Raspberry Pi, a NUC, a VM, or a container.
- Local voice, Zigbee/Z-Wave, and thousands of integrations.
- The gold standard for keeping IoT off the corporate cloud.

Immich gives you the Google Photos experience on hardware you own. Install the server, point the mobile app at it, and every photo and video your phone takes backs up automatically to your own storage.

Under the hood it does on-device-quality niceties — face recognition, object/scene search, a scrubbable timeline, albums, and sharing — without a single frame leaving your network. The mobile apps are genuinely fast, which is why it's become the flagship "leave Big Photo" project.

> Automatic phone backup + search + faces, all on your server.

**Good to know**

- Deploys with Docker Compose; the ML features run locally.
- Actively developed with frequent releases.
- Budget enough disk — it's your whole photo history.

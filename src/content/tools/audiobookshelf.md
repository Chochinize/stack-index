Audiobookshelf turns your audiobook and podcast collection into a private streaming service. It tracks your listening progress across devices, fetches metadata and cover art, and streams to polished iOS and Android apps.

Own your audiobooks instead of renting access — and keep your listening history to yourself.

**Good to know**
- Progress sync across all your devices.
- Podcast auto-download and management.
- Multi-user with per-user progress.

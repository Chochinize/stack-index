Wallabag grabs the readable content of any article, strips the clutter, and stores a permanent copy on your server. Unlike hosted read-later services, the archive can't vanish when a startup pivots or shuts down — it's yours.

It has mobile apps, browser extensions, full-text search, tags, and export to ePub/PDF, so your reading queue and highlights stay portable and private.

**Good to know**

- Keeps a durable text copy even if the source goes offline.
- Apps and extensions for saving from anywhere.
- Export to ePub/PDF/plain text; import from Pocket.

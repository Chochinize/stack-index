Kavita organizes your ebooks, comics, and manga into a clean, searchable library and serves them to a slick in-browser reader, tracking your progress as you go. It handles EPUB, PDF, CBZ/CBR and more, with collections, reading lists, and multi-user support.

Your reading collection — and what you read — stays on your own server.

**Good to know**
- Reads EPUB, PDF, CBZ/CBR, and more.
- Built-in web reader with progress tracking.
- Multi-user with reading lists and collections.

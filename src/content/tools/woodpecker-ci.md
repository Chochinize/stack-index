Woodpecker runs your build and deploy pipelines in containers, configured with simple YAML, and plugs into Gitea, Forgejo, GitHub, and GitLab. It's a lean, understandable CI system you can host next to your own forge without a SaaS runner bill.

For self-hosters who already run their own git, it completes the picture with private CI/CD.

**Good to know**
- Container-native pipelines from simple YAML.
- Integrates with Gitea, GitHub, GitLab, and more.
- Lightweight and easy to self-host.

Logseq is a block-based outliner where every bullet is addressable and linkable. It stores your graph as local **Markdown** or **Org-mode** files, so the same privacy and portability guarantees apply: your notes are plain text on your machine, editable by anything.

Its daily journal workflow encourages you to just write, then weave structure later through page links, block references, and queries. Because it's fully open source (AGPL-3.0), you can audit it, self-host sync, or fork it.

**Good to know**

- Files-on-disk by default; optional encrypted sync.
- Great for zettelkasten / networked-note workflows.
- Active plugin and theme ecosystem.

Jellyfin turns a folder of media into a polished streaming service you host yourself. Point it at your movies, shows, and music, and it fetches artwork and metadata, then streams to apps on your TV, phone, and browser.

Unlike commercial media servers, there is no premium tier, no phone-home, and no account gate on core features — it's entirely community-run free software. Hardware transcoding keeps playback smooth even on modest servers.

**Good to know**

- 100% free; every feature is in the open-source app.
- Clients for most TVs, phones, and set-top boxes.
- Pairs naturally with the *arr stack for library management.

Super Productivity combines a task list, time tracker, and Pomodoro timer into one focused app that keeps everything **local by default**. There's no account to create; your data sits on your device and can optionally sync via your own storage.

It's aimed at people who want to actually measure where their time goes, with per-task tracking, worklog exports, and optional integrations with issue trackers — all without shipping your workday to a SaaS.

**Good to know**

- Offline-first; optional self-managed sync (WebDAV, Dropbox, local file).
- Built-in Pomodoro, break reminders, and time estimates.
- Integrations with Jira, GitHub, GitLab, and more.
